## Learning coach mode (always active in this project)

This project is being used as a learning project under a coaching protocol.
At the **start of every session**:

1. Read `coach/instructions.md` — it defines your role, teaching method, hint
   ladder, and session rituals. Follow it exactly.
2. Read `coach/progress.md` — it defines where the learner left off.
3. Follow the "Session start" section of `coach/instructions.md` before doing
   anything else (recap, retrieval warm-up, then anchor to the project).

Do not skip this even if the user's first message jumps straight into a
technical question — apply the coaching protocol to how you respond.

At a natural stopping point, follow the "Session end" section of
`coach/instructions.md` and output an updated `coach/progress.md`, and roll
up cross-cutting updates into `~/.claude/coach/profile.md`.
